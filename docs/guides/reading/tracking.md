# Tracking

Track your reading progress from OpenComic.

----

## Supported sites

- [AniList](https://anilist.co/)
- [MyAnimeList](https://myanimelist.net/)
- [MangaBaka](https://mangabaka.org/)
- [Bangumi](https://bgm.tv/)

All four integrations support chapter and volume progress.

![Manual tracking](/img/docs/reading/tracking/tracking-1.png)

----

## Connect a tracking site

1. Open a comic and open the **Tracking** menu in the reader.
2. Select a site and follow its sign-in or authorization steps.
3. Search for the comic and select the matching entry.

Once connected, OpenComic can synchronize your chapter or volume progress with that site. You can mark frequently used sites as favorites in the Tracking menu.

----

## Manual tracking

If OpenComic cannot detect the volume or chapter you are currently reading, you can track it manually.
